# The Underdwellers — Worldbuilder's Notes

*Full working notes on a homebrew subterranean species: a reimagining of "dwarves" as blind, invertebrate cave-dwellers, built from biology outward rather than from folklore inward. A trimmed DM-facing pitch is extracted from this document separately; this version keeps the full reasoning, alternatives considered, and open threads.*

---

## 1. Design Premise

Steer away from the Tolkien/Viking-derived "short stocky human" dwarf entirely. Keep the *cultural* core of dwarves — stonework, permanence, craft, memory, clan — but rebuild the body and senses from real biological principles for a species that:
- Lives entirely underground, in total darkness
- Is roughly 1.5 m tall
- Manufactures tools and has a genuine culture
- Is not required to be a vertebrate or a mammal

Opening the design to invertebrates was the key unlock — arthropods and mollusks already have real-world precedent for every non-visual sense needed, often at a level of sophistication that outperforms the mammalian equivalent in the right niche.

---

## 2. Why Not Vision, and Why Not Just Echolocation?

Background reasoning (kept for consistency, not for the pitch doc):
- Vision is evolutionarily cheap and near-universal because light is available almost everywhere and even a crude photoreceptor pays off immediately. It's why eyes evolved 40+ times independently.
- Echolocation is expensive (dedicated sound production + high-resolution hearing + fast neural timing) and only pays off when vision fails (darkness, murk, caves) — hence rare, but not impossible: bats, toothed whales, oilbirds, swiftlets, some shrews/tenrecs.
- Other lineages facing the same "no light" problem often evolved *other* active senses instead of echolocation, because they already had a cheaper alternative: weakly electric fish (electroreception), pit vipers (infrared), fish lateral lines (passive water displacement sensing). This is the direct precedent for giving Underdwellers a non-echolocation solution: **substrate vibration sensing + chemoreception**, mirroring real spider/insect biology rather than bat/whale biology.

## 3. Physiology

- Large, upright, pillworm/isopod-like body plan, chitinous exoskeleton, ~1.5 m tall.
- **Lower limbs**: multiple pairs, dedicated to locomotion.
- **Upper limbs**: four pairs, thin and arthropod-like — functionally closer to *fingers* than to arms or hands. Individually dexterous but weak; two-handed/heavy tasks require most or all pairs working together. (Explicitly **not** a source of mechanical combat advantage — flavor only, see Section 8.)
- **Head**: no true eyes. Vestigial photoreceptors sense light/dark only, no image formation — flinches from bright daylight (glare/overload, not true blindness-pain). Several long, mobile antennae, the primary organs of chemoreception and close-range touch.
- Likely retains some gravity/balance organ (statocyst-equivalent) — works identically above and below ground, giving them excellent footing on cliffs, rubble, and ledges even on the surface.

---

## 4. Senses (full detail)

**Tremorsense (primary, tiered by range):**
- 0–60 ft: precise location of anything in contact with the ground/connected solid surface.
- 61–120 ft: vague — direction and rough distance only, for Medium+ or forceful movement; disadvantage on attacks based only on this; stealthy/small movement doesn't register.
- 120+ ft: only very large-scale events register as "something happened, that direction."

**Scent (secondary, for anything not grounded):** flying/waterborne/otherwise ungrounded creatures are invisible to tremorsense but detectable by antenna-scent within ~30 ft (wind-affected) — presence and rough bearing only, never precise location. Scentless creatures (constructs, some undead, oozes) are undetectable by this and are a good "genuinely scary" enemy type for this species specifically.

**Antenna Probe (close-range precision, risk/reward):** extending antennae within 5 ft (one body length) resolves full, precise information on a target — but probing something not already precisely located (e.g. a flying enemy known only by scent) risks a free reaction attack, since it's reaching blind into range. Central tactical decision loop: advance and confirm, or hold back and infer.

Underground, this sensory suite is total and precise. On the surface, it fragments — see Section 7.

## 5. Communication

**Kin-tongue** — chemo-tactile "first" language, antenna-to-antenna, close range only. Develops first ontologically (how parents bond with infants) but structurally *cannot* be written down: it's continuous (gradient-based, blended, decaying) rather than discretized, so nothing about it survives being "frozen." Biologically impossible for non-antennae species to ever learn or perceive, even in principle — not a translation problem, a hardware problem (see Section 9, Art).

**Undercant** — secondary tap/scratch vibration language, works at range through solid surfaces. Because it's made of discrete pulses (a tap either happens or doesn't), it *can* be frozen into permanent form — grooves and notches pressed or carved into stone, clay, or waxed material, read by dragging an antenna across the surface. This becomes their **writing system**. A dwarven scholarly tradition could have an explicit in-world essay on "why we do not write scent," reasoning through exactly this asymmetry.

Notes on the writing system:
- Physical form: wet clay pressed by a tool that mimics the tapping limb; slate/stone chiseled for permanent/archival texts; a scroll-equivalent could be a workable ribbon material for erasable drafts vs. stone for "publication."
- Reading is an active physical motion (dragging an antenna), so reading speed is personal and variable — a skilled reader could parallel-process by dragging multiple antennae over different passages simultaneously, an alien reading behavior with no human equivalent.
- Script may not be linear left-to-right — could spiral or run helically, echoing the shape of a bore-hole, so reading and mining could share a learned motor pattern.
- Possible base-8/base-4 numeral system, counting by limb-pairs.
- A scent-marking "signature" or emotional-tone marginalia layer could sit alongside (not replace) the literal Undercant grooves — a two-channel document, literal meaning in the grooves, emotional/authorship context in a scent-daub read near (not on) the text.

They cannot produce or perceive spoken Common or other airborne languages without external help (translator or prosthesis — Section 7).

## 6. Society & Culture

**Clan-holds, not cities.** Kin-tongue's close-range requirement means real intimacy is necessarily physical — a clan-hold is a cluster of chambers where kin live close enough to touch daily. Larger settlements are pragmatically several clan-holds sharing infrastructure (forges, shafts, water) rather than a single unified populace; trust runs out at the edge of who you've physically touched. Strangers — even other Underdwellers from a different hold — start as functional foreigners.

**The Wall-Debt (memory-as-law).** Every clan-hold keeps a Reckoning Wall near the hearth-chamber: debts, wrongs, oaths, and grievances physically notched in Undercant, meant to last as long as the stone does. Nothing is forgotten because nothing *can* be — it isn't oral legend that drifts over generations, it's an unforgeable groove. Reading the wall (running antennae over the family's notches) is a Kin-tongue social obligation before major rites: coming of age, marriage, taking an apprentice, leaving for the surface. A grudge can pass down for generations exactly like classic dwarven grudge-lore, but here it's literal and sensable rather than legendary — very hard to talk your way out of, very easy to use as a plot hook (an ancestor's unpaid debt to the humans who stopped showing up).

**Theme, stated explicitly:** classic dwarves resist change and are hurt by the resistance (hoarding, isolation, grudges calcifying into ruin). Underdwellers invert the *mechanism* but keep the *wound*: they've built an entire civilization on the premise that some things should be permanent and unchangeable — carved once, true forever. The surface breaks that premise immediately: nothing up there holds still to be carved, meaning has to be caught in real time and lost again, a prosthesis fails in rain, comprehension must be rebuilt painstakingly instead of settled once. Their tragic flaw isn't stubbornness, it's that they are permanence-lovers in a world that will not hold still long enough for them to carve anything into it. Worth keeping this explicit in fluff text rather than implicit, so surface-adaptation reads as *the* dwarvish flaw reinvented, not an incidental disability mechanic.

## 7. Diet & Metabolism

Ectothermic, likely lower baseline metabolism than a similarly-sized mammal — needs dramatically less food, can go days between meals without harm.

**Underground food web**, built on the two real strategies deep caves actually use (no sunlight = no primary production via photosynthesis, need a substitute):
- **Cultivated chemosynthetic mats**: bacteria grown in warm, mineral-rich seep chambers (real-world precedent: Movile Cave, Romania) — their staple crop, "wheat field" equivalent. Raw mat is mildly toxic; cooking is what makes it safe, giving fire a genuine survival function rather than being decorative.
- **Fungiculture on imported detritus**: mushrooms/molds grown on guano, driftwood, leaf litter washed or blown in from the surface — the "vegetable" tier, real-world precedent in actual disused mine fungiculture.
- **Cave livestock**: blind fish, cave shrimp/crayfish, cave crickets; bat-roosts kept for guano fertilizer and occasional meat (a nice thematic echo — farming the surface world's other blind sensory specialist).
- **Geophagy**: minerals eaten directly, using the same antennae-taste already used for ore-prospecting — "checking a vein" and "having a snack" can be the same motion.
- **Surface imports** (grain, dried fruit, preserved meat): rare luxury, valuable specifically because — unlike everything else on this list — it spoils. Functions like historical salt/spice trade.

**Economic consequence**: the underground food web is sufficient for survival but not abundance — a slow-burning vulnerability rather than a constant famine, well-suited to a culture that's organized itself around scarcity rather than one perpetually starving. Makes the "humans stopped showing up" inciting incident a food-security question, not just a diplomatic one.

**Food as ceremony.** If one meal sustains someone for the better part of a week, eating is not a daily habit but an event. A clan-hold plausibly gathers for a full communal meal only weekly (or less) — and that gathering is the natural venue for reading the Reckoning Wall aloud, swearing oaths, binding apprentices, settling debts before witnesses. Food, law, and family collapse into one recurring institution rather than three separate ones. ("Sunday lunch with the family" as *the* central social institution, not a quaint aside.)

**Adventuring note**: an Underdweller PC likely doesn't need daily rations like human companions — small humanizing detail, minor logistics quirk if rations are tracked at the table.

## 8. Magic & Communication

**Telepathy is the one clean workaround for their sensory isolation.** Mind-to-mind magic (innate telepathy traits, *Rary's Telepathic Bond*, the *Telepathy* spell, *Sending*) transmits thought directly, bypassing every sense — no tremorsense, antennae, ears, or eyes involved. The standard rule for these effects requires only that the target understand *some* language of its own, not that it share the caster's — and Undercant/Kin-tongue both count, so an Underdweller clears that bar easily. A human wizard with the right spell could converse fully and fluently with an Underdweller, no translator, no prosthesis.

This makes telepathy-granting magic disproportionately precious to this species — a treasured relic, not a curiosity, since it's the only known method giving them unmediated contact with a surface-dweller. Strong quest-reward material.

**What telepathy doesn't replicate**: the sensory experience of Kin-tongue itself (antenna-contact, pheromone nuance) — only the meaning of words. Even perfect telepathic fluency with a human would still feel like talking through glass compared to talking with kin. This preserves Kin-tongue's intimacy and "unknowable to outsiders" status even in a setting where telepathy magic exists.

**Comprehend Languages / Tongues**: keyed to spoken/written language, likely wouldn't crack Undercant or Kin-tongue without a specific DM ruling. Open question worth deciding at session zero: does Undercant (vibration through stone, not sound through air) count as "spoken" for that spell's purposes?

## 9. Art & Culture

Central organizing idea: art forms built on *distance senses* (sight, hearing-through-air) either vanish or transform beyond recognition; art forms built on *contact/vibration/chemical* senses survive close to intact, or intensify into something richer than the human equivalent. A few forms become genuinely unknowable to outsiders — not "untranslated," but structurally impossible for a human to ever perceive, even in principle.

**Sculpture** — survives closest to unchanged, but shifts from a *distance* art to a *contact* art. Meant to be touched, not viewed from a respectful few feet back — repeated touching by many antennae over generations becomes part of the work itself, the way a cathedral saint's foot gets worn shiny by pilgrims. Popular pieces might grow smoother and more abstract over centuries purely from being loved.

**Drawing/painting** — done in pastes of differing mineral composition rather than pigment/color, read by antenna taste/texture rather than sight. Palette is organized by mineral family and texture, not hue — a "warm" work might use sulfur compounds, a "somber" one iron oxide, independent of what it looks like to a human eye. A quietly tragic worldbuilding beat: an Underdweller masterpiece could sit in a human museum dismissed as a drab grey smear, while to its intended audience it's rich and complex.

**Literature/poetry** — closest to unchanged conceptually, since it's built on the already-writable Undercant. The poetry/music boundary, blurry even for humans, may not exist for them at all: since Undercant carving is simultaneously their writing *and* their percussion, a "poem" and a "drumbeat" might be a single continuous art form rather than two adjacent ones.

**Music** — vibration-based, blending percussion and "lyric" into one channel, effectively imperceptible to unaided humans. Genuinely alien in a deep way: substrate (solid stone) has different acoustic physics than air, so their harmony/rhythm theory (if it exists) would be mathematically unrelated to human music theory, not just differently tuned.

**Theater** — plausible, built on Undercant and tremorsense. A more intimate form, with performers within antennae-reach of the audience, could exist but sit at or past the edge of social propriety — likely governed by strict norms. Neither form is comprehensible to an unaided human audience. Possible overlap with law/memory: a formal re-enactment of a grudge or debt performed at a communal meal, functioning as both entertainment and social record — "going to the show" and "reading the news" as the same event.

**Architecture** — arguably the art form most reshaped by their sensory package. A human building is designed to be *seen* (facades, skylines, sightlines); an Underdweller structure is designed to be *heard and felt* — a hall's beauty judged primarily by how a tap resonates and travels through it, the way a cathedral's beauty is judged by light. Structural engineering and aesthetic judgment might be nearly the same skill for them (a resonant wall and a structurally sound wall evaluated by the same antenna-test), unlike the separated disciplines they are for humans. Echoes the "vibration is inherently digital, that's why it became writing" logic — vibration may unify architecture, music, and structural engineering into one discipline for this species.

**Scent-composition (new, wholly untranslatable art form)** — built entirely on Kin-tongue, performed antenna-to-antenna, somewhere between perfumery and poetry. This is the one genuinely unknowable art form: not merely undecoded, but structurally impossible for a human to ever perceive even in principle, absent the organ to receive it (unlike vibration, which a crude prosthesis can at least partially translate). Likely treated as sacred/private, restricted to kin, never exported, never appears in translated form because there is nothing to translate it *into* — the one art form that stays entirely theirs.

**Dance, reframed.** Choreography meant to be *watched* makes no sense without distance vision — but choreography meant to be *felt through the floor by an audience in contact with the ground* works well: a stamping, rhythmic performance that's simultaneously dance, music, and a shared tremorsense experience, where the audience doesn't watch the dancers, they feel them through the stone everyone is touching together.

## 10. 5e Mechanical Notes (full working, see extracted pitch for the clean version)

- ASI: Str +2, Con +1. Medium size, Powerful Build (carry/lift as one size larger). Speed 25 ft (30 ft + advantage on initiative underground/in contact with stone on 2+ sides — "Tunnel Fighter").
- Tremorsense tiered 0–60/61–120/120+ ft as in Section 4; replaces darkvision entirely (none).
- Scent sense for ungrounded creatures, Antenna Probe bonus action for close-range precision at risk of a reaction attack, as in Section 4.
- No Airborne Hearing: auto-fail hearing checks unless via solid-surface contact or Sonic Prosthesis (disadvantage on hearing checks and sound-direction checks while worn).
- Vestigial Sight: light/dark only, otherwise treated as blind. Sunlight Sensitivity: disadvantage on attacks/other-sense Perception in direct sunlight.
- Many-Limbed: flavor only, explicitly no mechanical bonus (four thin, finger-like limb pairs; two-handed tasks need most/all of them).
- Stonecunning (as Dwarf, doubled proficiency on stonework History checks).
- Languages: Undercant (tap/vibration, usable by trained outsiders) and Kin-tongue (touch/chemical, functionally restricted to the species or specially trained creatures).
- Class notes: no class excluded. Barbarian/Fighter/Monk fit the physical build (flavor, not mechanical, multi-limb bracing). Rogue is powerful underground, sharply limited above (built-in roleplay tension). Ranger reflavors tracking as scent-casting. Casters need no reflavor at all mechanically — verbal components as self-generated taps, spellbooks as tactile grooved pages. Bard is a fun edge case (a felt/drummed performance needing translation for non-Undercant audiences).
- Open DM discussion points: tremorsense's tiering is more generous than flat 30-ft blindsight, balanced by total blindness to ungrounded threats — flag flying/shipboard/rope-bridge encounters in advance. Sonic Prosthesis is gear (AC 10, 1 hp), fails in weather, delivers noise before meaning — comprehension should be a slow narrative arc, not an instant fix. The Reckoning Wall is a strong backstory generator — worth asking whether a PC left home with an open debt, an inherited grudge, or an oath the party's mission might close out.
